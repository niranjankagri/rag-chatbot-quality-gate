## M1 Membership plans and prices

Northpeak offers three membership plans. The monthly plan costs 59 euros per month. The annual plan costs 590 euros, paid once for twelve months. The off-peak plan costs 39 euros per month and is valid Monday to Friday before 16:00. Students with a valid student card get 15 percent off the monthly and annual plans.

## M2 Freezing a membership

A membership can be frozen for up to 3 months per calendar year, for example during an injury or a trip. A freeze costs 5 euros per month. The freeze must be requested at the front desk or in the app at least 7 days before it starts.

## M3 Cancelling a membership

The monthly plan can be cancelled with 30 days notice, in the app or at the front desk. The annual plan has a 14 day cooling-off period with a full refund; after that it is not refunded, but it can be transferred to another person once for a fee of 20 euros.

## M4 Payment methods

Memberships are paid by SEPA direct debit or credit card. Day passes can also be paid in cash at the front desk. A failed direct debit is retried once after 5 days, and the membership is paused if the second attempt fails.
