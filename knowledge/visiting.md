---
title: Visiting the gym
access: public
---

## V1 Opening hours

The gym is open Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 23:00, and on Saturday and Sunday from 09:00 to 21:00. The last entry is 60 minutes before closing time.

## V2 Holiday opening

The gym is closed on 25 December and 1 January. On 24 December and 31 December it closes early, at 16:00. On all other public holidays the weekend hours apply.

## V3 Day passes and visit cards

A day pass costs 18 euros for adults and 12 euros for children under 14. A 10-visit card costs 160 euros and is valid for one year from the first visit. Shoe rental costs 4 euros and harness rental costs 3 euros.

## V4 Bringing a guest

Members can bring one guest per calendar month at half the day pass price. The member must stay with the guest, and the guest signs the safety waiver at the front desk before climbing.

## V5 Parking and getting here

There are 40 free parking spaces behind the building, and bicycle racks at the entrance. The tram stop Northpeak Halle is a 3 minute walk away.
