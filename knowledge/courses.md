title: Courses and safety
access: public

## C1 Introduction course

The introduction course has 2 sessions of 2 hours and costs 45 euros. It teaches knots, belaying with a tube device and falling safely. Anyone who wants to rope climb without a coach must have finished the introduction course or pass the belay check.

## C2 Belay check

Climbers who learned to belay elsewhere can take the belay check instead of the course. It is free, takes about 15 minutes, and can be done at the front desk without booking. A climber who fails the check may repeat it after 7 days.

## C3 Kids club

The kids club is for children aged 6 to 12 and meets every Saturday at 10:00 for 90 minutes. A term of 10 sessions costs 120 euros. Climbing shoes are included in the kids club.

## C4 Climbing rules

Chalk balls or liquid chalk only; loose chalk powder is not allowed. Auto-belays must be clipped to the belay loop before every climb. Nobody may stand on the bouldering mats below a climber.

## C5 Children and teenagers

Children under 14 must be supervised by an adult at all times. Teenagers aged 14 to 17 may climb alone if a parent has signed the consent form and they have passed the belay check.
